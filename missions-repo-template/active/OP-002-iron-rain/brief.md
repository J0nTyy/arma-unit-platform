# Operation Iron Rain

## Situation

Enemy mechanized forces are pushing west through the Nadbór corridor. The
Topolin logistics hub is the only supply point still feeding our forward
positions, and the bridge behind it was crippled in yesterday's shelling.

## Mission

Hold Topolin against mechanized assaults until the engineer detachment
completes bridge repairs, then conduct an orderly withdrawal across it.

## Objectives

- **Primary:** Hold the Topolin depot perimeter until repairs are complete.
- **Secondary:** Keep at least one supply truck operational.

## Rules / Restrictions

- The depot's fuel farm must not be used as a firing position (it will cook off).

## Equipment

Defensive stores are pre-staged: mines, static AT, and sandbag kits at the
depot. Resupply arrives with each lull between waves.

## Execution

Three assault waves are expected, escalating from motorized infantry to a
combined mechanized push. Between waves, elements may reposition and re-arm.

## Extraction

On bridge completion, withdraw by vehicle column across the river. Rear
guard holds the near bank until the column is across.

## Notes for Mission Makers

Wave timing is driven by the repair progress variable, not the clock —
slower engineer play stretches the mission naturally.
