# Operation Blackout

## Situation

CSAT forces have established an integrated air-defence network across
north-eastern Altis. Three radar installations near Sofia give their SAM
batteries early warning coverage over the entire gulf. Allied air operations
in the region are grounded until the network is blinded.

## Background

Two previous SEAD strikes were aborted after the radar network detected the
ingress. Command has authorized a ground raid under cover of darkness —
destroy the radar hardware directly and slip out before the local garrison
can react.

## Mission

Infiltrate the Sofia coastal sector by boat, destroy the primary radar
installation at Hill 143, and exfiltrate via the southern beach before 0500.

## Objectives

- **Primary:** Destroy the radar installation at Hill 143.
- **Secondary:** Capture the CSAT signals officer believed to be quartered in
  the compound below the hill.
- **Optional:** Destroy the backup generator to delay network restoration.

## Rules / Restrictions

- No engagement before the first demolition charge is set unless compromised.
- Civilian structures in Sofia are strictly off-limits for demolitions.
- NVG discipline: white light is a fail condition for stealth bonuses.

## Equipment

Standard night-raid loadout. Demolitions carried by the engineer team;
suppressed weapons for all infiltration elements. Boats provided at the
start point.

## Intelligence

- Garrison strength estimated at platoon level, low alert state.
- One BTR-K patrol on the coastal road, 20-minute loop.
- The signals officer's quarters are marked on the map — expect a close
  protection detail of 2–4.

## Execution

Phase 1: beach insertion, 2 km night movement to the rally point.
Phase 2: simultaneous breach of the radar compound and officer quarters.
Phase 3: demolition, HVT secure, collapse to the southern beach.

## Extraction

Boats staged at grid 041022. Fallback extraction by helicopter at LZ Crow
if the beach is compromised.

## Notes for Mission Makers

The garrison alert state is scripted: it escalates if shots are fired before
the charges are set. Test both the stealth and loud branches before review.
