# Operation Name

## Situation

What is happening in the area of operations? Who is where, and why does it
matter right now?

## Background

Optional: the wider context. Delete this section if you don't need it.

## Mission

One paragraph: who does what, where, and by when. This is the sentence a
squad leader reads out loud.

## Objectives

Summarize the objectives in plain language (the structured version lives in
objectives.json — keep the two consistent).

## Rules / Restrictions

ROE, off-limits areas, required behaviours. Delete if none.

## Equipment

What players carry, what is provided in the field, any loadout restrictions.

## Intelligence

Enemy strength, patrols, known positions — what players are allowed to know
before the mission starts.

## Execution

The intended phases of the mission. It's fine if players improvise.

## Extraction

How the mission ends and how everyone gets home (or doesn't).

## Notes for Mission Makers

Anything reviewers/other makers should know: scripted triggers, performance
notes, things to test. Players don't need to read this section.
